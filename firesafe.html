Common questions

What people ask before they buy.

Which size should I get?

For most crypto holders the SentrySafe SFW082GTC or SFW123GDC ($120-180) is enough — fits a hardware wallet, two YubiKeys, a steel plate, and the household documents. Don't size up beyond what you'll actually use; bigger safes are heavier (which is good for theft resistance) but harder to bolt down and reposition.

Bolt-down or freestanding?

Always bolt down. A 100 lb safe sounds heavy until two people with a dolly take it out the door. Most safes ship with bolt-down hardware and the floor reinforcement is straightforward. Bolting cuts opportunistic theft to roughly zero — only a determined, equipped attacker with time and tools is a real threat once it's bolted.

Combination, key, or electronic lock?

Electronic with a backup key override. Pure combination dials are slower under stress and more prone to lockouts. Pure electronic without a backup key fails if the battery is dead and you need urgent access. The hybrid (electronic primary, physical key in a sealed envelope at a relative's house) is the right answer.

What goes inside?

Hardware wallet (in Faraday bag), steel seed plate, both YubiKeys, recovery codes printout, passport, birth certificate, property deeds, vehicle titles, life insurance documents. The safe is your cold storage AND your home-document insurance — the same box solves both.

Stand-alone or inside a closet?

Inside a closet, bolted to the floor or wall, behind something else. The harder it is to find, the less likely someone gets to even start trying. A safe in plain sight is a target marker; a safe behind shoes in a closet is invisible.
