What about my DeFi swaps and L2 bridges?

Supported. Koinly tracks Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche, Solana, Cosmos, and most major chains. DEX swaps on Uniswap, Sushi, Jupiter, etc. parse correctly. Bridges between L1/L2 are recognized as transfers, not taxable events.

What if I have NFTs or staking rewards?

Both handled. NFT mints, sales, and trades are tracked with cost basis. Staking rewards (ETH, SOL, ATOM, DOT, etc.) are tagged as ordinary income at fair market value on receipt — which is what the IRS requires.

Can I file directly from Koinly?

You don't file from Koinly directly — but you export the IRS-ready Form 8949 PDF or push it directly into TurboTax / H&R Block. If you use a CPA, the accountant-ready report is the format every crypto-experienced accountant I've worked with prefers.

How accurate is the cost-basis math?

Koinly supports FIFO, LIFO, HIFO, and average cost — pick the method your jurisdiction allows. It also flags potentially missing transactions (a withdrawal with no matching deposit elsewhere) so you can hunt down the gap before reporting. That gap-detection is the feature that saves me from wrong numbers.
