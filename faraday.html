01 — THE EXPOSURE

NFC and BLE leak constantly.

Anything wireless is a beacon. Phones ping cell towers every minute. Hardware wallets with Bluetooth (Ledger Nano X, Trezor Safe 5) advertise themselves continuously. Key fobs broadcast on demand. Anyone with the right scanner within range can fingerprint, track, or in some cases relay-attack the signal.

02 — THE BLOCK

Conductive fabric kills the signal.

A Faraday bag works on physics, not software. The metallic mesh inside the bag forms a Faraday cage — incoming radio waves induce currents in the mesh that cancel themselves out before reaching the device. Tested bags hit 80-100 dB attenuation, which means the signal coming out is roughly one-billionth of what went in.

Common questions

What people ask before adding one.

Do I really need this if I'm not paranoid?

Honest answer: most days, no. The day someone in the next hotel room is running a Bluetooth scanner, yes. The day relay attackers are working a parking lot, yes. The bag costs $25 and lives in a drawer until you travel — low cost, asymmetric protection. Treat it like the seatbelt of cold storage.

How is it different from a regular case?

A regular case is fabric and plastic — radio waves pass right through. A Faraday bag is lined with metallic mesh (typically copper or nickel-coated fabric). The mesh forms an electromagnetic cage that attenuates signals by 80-100 dB. You can't tell the difference by touch, but a quick test — try to make a phone call to the phone inside — proves it.

Will it block GPS too?

Yes. GPS is a one-way signal from satellites — when the device is in the bag, no GPS lock. Useful when you don't want a phone or wallet's location logged. Note: location can still be inferred from cell tower triangulation history before the bag was used, so this is forward protection, not retroactive.

Is this useful for travel?

Very. Hotel safes are not actually secure against a determined attacker — staff have master access. A Faraday bag inside a hotel safe means even if the wallet is found, it can't be powered on and probed remotely. For conferences (where Wi-Fi/Bluetooth attacks are common), keep the wallet in the bag until you actively need it.

Which size?

The key fob / wallet size ($20-30) fits a hardware wallet plus the recovery card. The phone size ($30-45) fits the wallet, a phone, and one or two extra items. The laptop size ($45-60) fits everything plus a 13-inch laptop. Most crypto holders are fine with the phone size.
