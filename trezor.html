Common questions

What traders ask before adding Trezor.

Which Trezor should I get?

For most holders the Trezor Safe 3 at $79 is the sweet spot — secure element, USB-C, supports 1,000+ tokens. The Safe 5 at $219 adds a touchscreen and Bluetooth. The legacy Model One is fine for Bitcoin-only users on a tight budget. Skip the original Trezor T unless you're a long-time user.

Trezor vs Ledger — which one?

Both are good. The right answer for serious holdings is both. Use Ledger's app ecosystem and broader chain support as the daily driver, Trezor's open-source firmware as the second-vendor backup. If forced to pick one: Ledger for convenience, Trezor for verifiable trust.

What about the 2022 Trezor phishing incident?

That was a phishing campaign against Trezor's email list, not a compromise of the device itself. No coins were stolen via the device. The lesson: never enter your seed phrase into anything that asks for it via email. The hardware itself remained uncompromised.

Does it support Solana, Cardano, the rest?

Yes — major chains all supported via Trezor Suite. Solana, Cardano, all the EVM chains, plus most ERC-20 / SPL tokens. Some long-tail altcoins are Ledger-only — check Trezor's coin list before buying if you hold something obscure.

Is it actually more secure than Ledger?

"More secure" depends on your threat model. Open-source firmware lets the public audit for backdoors — that's a real security property if you don't trust closed-source. Both vendors have secure-element chips and physical-confirmation transaction signing. The biggest security win is having two of them, not picking one over the other.
